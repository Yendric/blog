Meuby is an ERP system I built for [Patrima](https://patrima.be), a Belgian furniture store. It handles the store's day-to-day administration, from products and orders to invoicing. It also integrates with various furniture configurators (FurnPlan, iROM, Auping, JORI, Perfecta), allowing for easy imports of 3D configurations into the ERP.
