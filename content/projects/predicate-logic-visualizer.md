Predicate Logic Visualizer is a small Rust app that parses a predicate logic formula and visualizes its abstract syntax tree. Type a formula like `∀x (P(x) → ∃y Q(x, y))` and see how it breaks down.

You can try it at [predicate-logic.yendric.be](https://predicate-logic.yendric.be/).
